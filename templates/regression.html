Model Overview

This linear regression model estimates the expected energy drawn for electric vehicle charging sessions. The prediction is based on numerical variables from the dataset such as battery capacity, state of charge, distance to destination, queue time, station capacity, weather conditions, and fleet information.

Input Features
  • Battery Capacity
  • State of Charge
  • Energy Consumption Rate
  • Distance to Destination
  • Traffic Data
  • Charging Rate
  • Queue Time
  • Station Capacity
  • Energy Drawn
  • Session Start Hour
  • Fleet Size
  • Temperature
  • Wind Speed
  • Precipitation
  • Weekday

Interpretation
R2 Score shows how much of the variation in energy drawn is explained by the model.
Mean Absolute Error shows the average difference between the predicted energy drawn and the real energy drawn.
This model can support the recommendation system by estimating areas or conditions with higher expected energy demand.
Project Use

The regression result helps prioritize charging station planning. While the map recommends possible locations, the regression model helps estimate how much energy a charging session may require.
