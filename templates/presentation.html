Electric Mobility in Colombia

Charging infrastructure, connector types, and key deployment points across the national territory.

Infrastructure and Charging Levels

Level 1: Slow Charging

Uses alternating current (AC) with a standard household outlet (110V). Ideal for emergencies, electric motorcycles, or e-scooters. A full car charge takes between 12 to 20 hours.

Domestic

Level 2: Medium Charging

Alternating current (AC) at 220V. This is the most common standard in properly equipped households, shopping malls (Unicentro, Andino, Santafé), and public parking lots. Takes 4 to 8 hours.

Commercial / Residential

Level 3: Fast Charging

Direct current (DC). These high-power charging stations allow vehicles to reach up to an 80% charge in a 20 to 45-minute window. Essential for intercity highway travel.

💡 Key Regulatory Insight for Colombia

Under the Horizontal Property Law (Ley de Propiedad Horizontal) and current energy safety updates, installing private EV chargers in gated communities, condos, or residential buildings requires a formal electrical technical feasibility study and explicit approval from the co-owners' assembly to ensure grid stability.
